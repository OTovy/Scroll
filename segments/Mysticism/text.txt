Mysticism is a way of exploring reality through a non-logical lens. It leaves a large margin for error and opens the door to unplanned information that cannot be generated or received through logical means. It addresses blind spots.

It is a way of developing sensitivity, receptivity, and the ability to process information from reality through means other than the intellect.

Mystical terminology neutralizes the fixation of verbal thought and allows understanding to flow. When you give a name to something that is not yet understood or known, you can begin to move forward and work with it. Through practice, you gradually discover what it actually is.

The ideas behind mystical practices are childlike. When you practice through this prism, you are, in essence, rejuvenating your mind.
